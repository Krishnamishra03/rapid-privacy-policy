1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RapidCode Solution Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you use the RAPID mobile application and related services (the "App"). RAPID is a school attendance and student-safety platform used by schools, their staff (teachers, drivers, guards) and parents/guardians.

We provide the App on behalf of the school that licenses it. The school is the primary owner of the student and parent data processed through the App. By using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Who this Policy covers

  • School staff — teachers, bus drivers/attendants, security guards and administrators.
  • Parents / guardians — who track and manage their child's school transport and attendance.
  • Students — whose attendance and safe pickup/drop is recorded. Student accounts are set up and managed by the school and the parent, not by the student directly.

3. Information we collect

a. Account & profile information

  • Name, role (teacher / driver / parent / guard / admin), school code, mobile number, and (if provided) email.
  • For students: name, class and section, admission/roll number, and the assigned bus/route.
  • Parent/guardian name and contact details, and the link to their child.

b. Location information

  • Precise GPS location of the bus during an active trip, used to show parents the live position of the bus and to confirm safe boarding/drop.
  • Background location is used only for the driver/attendant device during an active trip, so bus tracking continues when the app is not on screen. It is not collected when no trip is running.

c. Camera and face (biometric) information

  • The App uses the device camera for QR scanning and for face recognition.
  • Face data is used to (i) mark student attendance / boarding and (ii) verify the identity of the person collecting a student at pickup, for safety. Face registration is done with the knowledge of the school and parent.
  • We treat face data as sensitive personal data. See Section 6.

d. Device & technical information

  • Device model, operating-system version, app version, and a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver alerts.
  • Basic log data needed to keep the service secure and working.

We do not knowingly collect payment card numbers, bank details, or government ID numbers through the App.

4. How we use information

  • Record and show student attendance and safe boarding/drop.
  • Let parents see their child's trip status and the live bus location on a map.
  • Verify, at pickup, that a student is handed to an authorised person.
  • Send push notifications (e.g. bus arriving, boarded, dropped, go-home/arrival plans, emergencies).
  • Enable driver duty check-in and emergency SOS alerts.
  • Keep the service secure, prevent misuse, and provide support.

5. Legal basis & consent

We process this information to perform the service requested by the school and the parent, and on the basis of consent given by the school and the parent/guardian. Where face/biometric data is used, it is processed only with the consent obtained by the school/parent. You may withdraw consent as described in Section 9.

6. Face / biometric data

  • Face data is used only for attendance and pickup-safety verification within the App.
  • We do not sell face/biometric data and do not use it for advertising or any purpose unrelated to student safety.
  • Face data is stored securely and access is restricted to the student's school and authorised processing needed to run the feature.
  • Face data is deleted when the student leaves the school, when the school/parent requests deletion, or when it is no longer needed for the purpose above (see Section 7 and 9).

7. Data retention

We keep information for as long as the student/user is active with the school and as required to provide the service, then delete or anonymise it within a reasonable period, unless a longer period is required by law or by the school's records policy. Trip and attendance logs may be retained by the school for its own record-keeping.

8. How we share information

  • With the school that licenses the App — it is the owner of the student/parent data.
  • Service providers that help us run the App, under confidentiality obligations:
    • Google Firebase (push notifications, app infrastructure) — see Google's Privacy Policy.
    • Google Maps / Directions (to display routes and the bus location on a map).
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of a child or any person.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

9. Your rights & choices

  • Access, correct, or request deletion of your (or your child's) information.
  • Withdraw consent, including for face/biometric data.
  • Control device permissions (camera, location) in your phone settings; note some features will not work without them.

To make a request, contact your school or email us at [privacy@yourdomain.com]. We will respond within a reasonable time. For student data, requests are handled together with the school.

10. Children's data

RAPID processes information about students, who may be minors, strictly for school attendance and transport safety, and only under the authority and consent of the school and the parent/guardian. We do not direct advertising to children and do not use children's data for any purpose other than the services described here.

11. App permissions we request

  • Camera — QR scanning and face recognition for attendance and pickup verification.
  • Location (precise) — live bus tracking during a trip; driver check-in.
  • Background location (driver/attendant only) — keep tracking during an active trip when the app is off screen.
  • Notifications / Internet — deliver alerts and communicate with the school server.
